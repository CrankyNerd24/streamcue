StreamCue Support

Last updated: September 2026

StreamCue is an app for tracking TV shows and films — what's airing, where it streams, and whether it's free. If something isn't working right, start here.

Common questions

Nothing shows up under "Where to watch."

Streaming availability is specific to a country. Check Settings → Streaming availability and make sure it's set to the right one — changing it refreshes your shows automatically.

How do I share my list with someone in my household?

In Settings, tap "Invite to household list" and send the invite link to whoever you want sharing the list. Once they accept it, items either of you add to the household list appear for both of you — each is an independent copy, so removing one later from either list doesn't affect the other.

I was invited to a household list but don't see anything on it.

Make sure you tapped Accept on the invite link, then fully close and reopen the app. If you still don't see anything after that, email us below and let us know which version you're on (Settings → About).

The app won't launch after an update.

This is rare, but if it happens, deleting and reinstalling the app usually resolves it — your list lives in your own iCloud account, not on the device, so it comes right back.

Everything else

For anything not covered here — a bug, a feature idea, or a question about how your data is handled — see the privacy policy or get in touch directly.
